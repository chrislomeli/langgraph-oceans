# Athletic Strength & Conditioning: Rehab-Safe Blueprint


### Bench
- **Seated Arnold Press — 3 sets × 10 reps**
- **Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row — 3 sets × 8–10 reps**
- **Single-Arm Dumbbell Row — 3 sets × 10 reps per side**
- **Dumbbell Skull Crushers — 3 sets × 10–12 reps**

### Stand
- **Dumbbell Bicep Curls — 3 sets × 10–12 reps**

### Floor
- **Floor Push-Ups — 3 sets to comfortable failure**
- **Planks** 30 x 3
- **90/90** 30m  seconds per side
- **Hollow Body Hold** 60 seconds plus


### Wall
- **Heel Raises** 3 X 12
- **Wall sit** 60 seconds plus
- **Deep squat** 3 X 30
